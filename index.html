What We Take & How It Works

If it is not hazardous, we can probably haul it. When in doubt, call us and ask.

What we take

  • Furniture Sofas, beds, tables, dressers, chairs and more.
  • Appliances Fridges, washers, dryers, stoves and dishwashers.
  • Mattresses & box springs Any size, from a single twin to a king set.
  • Boxes & bags of household junk Clutter, old clothes, broken toys, general cleanout junk.
  • Yard waste Branches, leaves, soil, broken fencing and garden debris.
  • Construction debris Drywall, lumber, tile, concrete and remodel waste.
  • Office & commercial junk Desks, chairs, retail fixtures and warehouse cleanouts.

What we cannot take

  • Hazardous materials Chemicals, paints, asbestos, fuel and similar items — we are not licensed to haul them.

Take hazardous waste to the Orange County household hazardous waste center instead: 6411 Oak Canyon, Irvine — open Tue–Sat, 9 AM–3 PM, free for OC residents.
